RESPONSIBLE USE

Designed as an academic prototype

Detect Now uses an EfficientNetB0 model trained and fine-tuned with balanced real and fake frames from the public DFDC Part 34 dataset. The model achieved 76.67% accuracy and 87.09% AUC on our separate testing set. It provides an experimental prediction rather than a forensic conclusion. Results may be affected by image quality, compression, lighting and unfamiliar manipulation techniques.
