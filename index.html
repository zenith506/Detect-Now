RESPONSIBLE USE

Designed as an academic prototype

Detect Now uses a YuNet face detector and an EfficientNetB0 classifier trained on cropped real and face-swapped portraits from two sources: Kaggle face-swap frames and swaps we made with the inswapper tool. On held-out test images it reached 83.1% accuracy on 1,894 Kaggle images (88.0% of real images correct, 78.2% of swaps caught) and about 83% on 322 inswapper images (94% real correct, 72% of swaps caught). The inswapper test uses the same tool as training, so it does not show how well the model handles swap tools it has never seen. It does not detect expression edits made by other AI tools, and roughly 6 to 12 in 100 real photos may be flagged as fake. The site shows a verdict of likely real (score below 15%), uncertain (15% to under 50%) or likely deepfake (50% and above). Scores are experimental and should not be treated as forensic proof.
